openDAW announcement decisions and style
========================================
Notes to remember how the user wants announcements written. Keep this updated.

Writing style (all channels)
• No dashes (em or en dashes). Use commas or periods instead.
• No semicolons. Split into two sentences, or use a comma.
• Plain language. No marketing speak (for example, avoid "The point:").

Per channel
• Discord: supports markdown. Big headline uses "# " and starts with "Update: ", in Title Case
  (keep brand casing like openDAW and Nextcloud). Links use markdown masked links [label](url).
  The tone is familiar, we talk to our community like friends. No business speech, no endless
  feature lists, no spec sheets. Short flowing paragraphs that tell what is fun about the thing.
  Use "### " sublines sparingly, only when a post really needs structure. No emoji.
• LinkedIn: professional. Hashtags at the end.
• Newsletter: focus on the schools angle. No greeting line (no "Hi ..."). No beta or pilot note.

Links
• openDAW: https://opendaw.studio
• Nextcloud manual: https://opendaw.studio/manuals/nextcloud

Feature framing (Nextcloud)
• Audience: schools and music classes first.
• Each student has their own Nextcloud account and own space, isolated from other students.
• Projects and assets stay on the school's own Nextcloud. openDAW talks to it directly. Nothing
  passes through openDAW servers.
• Samples and soundfonts are deduplicated, stored once and reused.
• openDAW is free and open.
• This is not a beta. Do not call it a beta.

Feature framing (Neon)
• A phase distortion synthesizer in the spirit of the Casio CZ series (1984). Say "in the spirit of",
  do not claim to be a Casio product.
• Education angle: phase distortion is teachable synthesis, one control morphs waveform and timbre,
  no filter involved.
• Real CZ-101 .syx patches load directly, thousands are freely available.
• Calibrated against measurements and recordings of real CZ hardware. Faithful, not "emulation
  perfect", avoid absolute claims.
• Manual: https://opendaw.studio/manuals/devices/instruments/neon
