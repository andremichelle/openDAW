## Summary
Naomi measured with the SDK (0.0.176, WASM engine) and an IAC repro page: with a one bar count-in, short notes pressed and released inside the count-in bar are never recorded, and a note held across the downbeat is written at position 0 with only the part after the downbeat (about 975 pulses of a half bar note). The audio recorder captures from before the count-in and keeps that head behind the region's `waveformOffset`, so a pickup can be revealed by trimming. The two recorders disagree.

André replied that dropping count-in notes is intended (the count-in is a cue), offered an engine preference to keep pickup notes, and pointed out that at bar 1 such notes would sit in negative time and never be heard. Naomi is asking her team. She had pictured the MIDI track starting at the first pickup note.

## Current state
1. `packages/studio/core/src/capture/RecordMidi.ts:178` to `:181`: a note on before the first take exists goes into `pendingNotes`. A note off while pending deletes it (`:203` to `:205`). When the first take opens at `engine.recordingStart`, `flushPendingNotes` writes every still held key at position 0 and measures its duration from the downbeat (`:131` to `:143`, `:152`).
2. Notes after the take start that arrive up to a sixteenth early are kept at position 0, earlier ones are dropped (`RecordMidi.ts:191`).
3. The take region starts at `quantizeFloor(position, beats)` (`RecordMidi.ts:150`), never earlier than the record start.
4. Audio: the take is anchored at `engine.recordingStart` and the captured head before it goes into `waveformOffset` (`packages/studio/core/src/capture/RecordAudio.ts:121` to `:135`).
5. Count-in length is the preference `recording.countInBars` (`packages/studio/adapters/src/engine/EnginePreferencesSchema.ts:35`). There is no pickup option.
6. No commits reference #493.

## Analysis
1. The behaviour is by design, not a bug. The open point is whether a pickup option is wanted.
2. A pickup option only makes sense when the record start is after bar 1. Then the region could start at the count-in start (or at the bar of the first pickup note) so pickup notes sit before the downbeat and are audible on playback. At bar 1 the notes would land at negative positions.
3. A MIDI region has no hidden head like the audio `waveformOffset`, so keeping pickup notes means starting the region earlier.
4. The held note at the downbeat losing its head is the same decision: with a pickup option it would start where it was pressed.

## Possible solution
1. Add a preference `recording.keepCountInNotes` (default off, current behaviour unchanged).
2. When on, store pending notes with their real time instead of only pitch and velocity, and when the first take opens, start the region at the bar containing the earliest pending note (clamped to position 0), placing notes at their transport position.
3. Clamp at bar 1: notes before position 0 are dropped as today.
4. Tests in `packages/studio/core/src/capture/RecordMidi.test.ts`: short pickup notes, held note across the downbeat, record start at bar 1 versus later bar, option off stays identical.

## Complexity
small. `packages/studio/core/src/capture/RecordMidi.ts`, the preferences schema in adapters, the preferences UI, tests.

## Open questions
1. For Naomi: does your team need pickup notes kept, given they only become audible when recording starts after bar 1?
2. For André: should the region start at the count-in start or at the bar of the first pickup note?
