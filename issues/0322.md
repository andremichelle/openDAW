## Summary
The reporter asks for Ambisonics and other multichannel immersive formats. Needed most: many output channels (64 minimum, 128 preferred), tracks with the same channel count (one multichannel recording per track), and basic cut and glue editing of such tracks. No comments.

## Current state
1. The whole engine is stereo. `AudioBuffer` holds only `left` and `right` (`crates/engine-env/src/audio_buffer.rs:11`), every device, bus and strip processes two channels.
2. The engine worklet outputs `numberOfChannels` on output 0 (2, or 2 per stem during export) plus an 8 channel monitor output (`packages/studio/core/src/EngineWorklet.ts:116`, `:146`).
3. Audio output device selection exists (`packages/app/studio/src/audio/AudioOutputDevice.ts`), but no multichannel output routing.
4. Nothing started for this issue.

## Analysis
Ambisonics needs N channel buffers through the whole graph: sample storage and playback of multichannel files, multichannel buses and strips, a panner or encoder per source, a decoder or binaural renderer for monitoring, and an output with many channels. Browsers limit the destination channel count to `AudioContext.destination.maxChannelCount`, which depends on the hardware, so 64 to 128 channels need a matching audio interface. Every stock device assumes stereo, and the device ABI passes two channel buffers. This touches the core of the engine.

## Possible solution
1. Decide the scope: either full multichannel (N channel graph) or a narrower path, for example binaural Ambisonics where sources are encoded and decoded to stereo inside one device or a WebCLAP, keeping the stereo graph.
2. For full support: make `AudioBuffer` channel count variable, extend the device ABI with channel layout negotiation, add multichannel audio files and regions, multichannel buses, output channel mapping, and export of multichannel files.
3. Write a plan before any code.

## Complexity
large. `crates/engine-env`, `crates/engine`, device ABI (`crates/abi`), all stock devices, sample storage, core worklets, mixer UI, export.

## Open questions
1. Is multichannel audio in scope for openDAW at all, or would a binaural Ambisonics device on the stereo graph be enough?
