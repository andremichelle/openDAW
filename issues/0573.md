## Summary
A Spielwerk script that plays long notes (the reporter's plays the chord track) followed by an Arpeggio: pressing Stop did not stop the arpeggio, it kept playing.

## Analysis
`runSpielwerk` (`packages/studio/core-wasm/src/script-spielwerk.ts`) released the notes it holds on a transport jump or when a note's end came due. Note ends are song positions and a stopped transport counts its own, so they never came due in time. The instrument kept the voices, and the Arpeggio kept the notes as held keys and arpeggiated them while stopped. Euclid already releases on the stop edge (`crates/stock-devices/device-euclid/src/lib.rs:303`).

## Fix
When the transport goes from playing to stopped, the runtime ends every held note and calls the script's `reset()`, as on a loop wrap. Test: `packages/studio/core-wasm/test/spielwerk-stop-release.test.ts` (with and without an Arpeggio).
