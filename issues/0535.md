## Summary
Riffle (SynthsBack-lab) draws its own compressor display from the compressor's live stream at `append(0)`, which carries `[in dB, reduction dB, out dB]`. In and out are peak meters with a 500 ms decay, so ghost notes and the gaps between hits do not show. The reporter asks for the input and output peak since the last frame.

## Current state
1. The compressor binds its editor broadcast with 3 floats at `crates/stock-devices/device-compressor/src/lib.rs:138`.
2. The 500 ms peak decay is set at `crates/stock-devices/device-compressor/src/lib.rs:140`. It was chosen on purpose for our compressor editor in commit 6367e5e71.
3. The Gate uses 10 ms peak followers (`crates/stock-devices/device-gate/src/lib.rs:51`).
4. A clarifying question was posted in the issue on 2026-11-09, asking whether a 10 ms decay is acceptable (a click half a frame before delivery arrives about 7 dB lower).

## Analysis
The slow decay suits our editor's meters but hides short events for a client that wants transient detail. Changing `append(0)` would change our own editor and break its look, so the fast values need their own address.

## Possible solution
Agreed with André:
1. Add a second pair of in/out peak followers with a 10 ms decay to the compressor state, like the Gate.
2. Broadcast them through the existing `abi::bind_broadcast` at `append(1)` with 2 floats `[in dB, out dB]`.
3. Leave `append(0)` unchanged. No new stream type.
4. Add a test in `packages/studio/core-wasm/test` that reads both addresses.

## Complexity
small. One stock device crate (`crates/stock-devices/device-compressor`), a rebuild of its wasm bundle, and one test.

## Open questions
1. For the reporter (already asked): is a 10 ms decay acceptable, given a click half a frame before delivery arrives about 7 dB lower, or is a true peak since the last read wanted (which needs a reset by the reader)?
