## Summary
`BrowserMonitor` panned with a `StereoPannerNode`. Chrome delivers a mono microphone as two identical channels, so hard pan carried twice the centre level, Firefox and Safari deliver one channel and got the equal power law. The channel strip uses a balance (`crates/engine-env/src/channel_strip.rs:116-117`).

## Fix
The monitor gain node upmixes to two channels (explicit, speakers), then a splitter, two gains and a merger apply the strip's balance. The `StereoPannerNode` stays as the chain's output at pan 0 (an exact pass-through for two channels), so `monitorPanNode` keeps its public type and the meter in the monitoring dialog keeps working. Test: `packages/studio/core/src/io/browser/BrowserMonitor.test.ts`.
