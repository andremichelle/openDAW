## Summary
With a forced mono capture and a stereo stream, the take and effects monitoring are the mixdown, direct monitoring carried the two channels as they arrived.

## Fix
Direct monitoring connects the monitoring mixdown (`monitorMixdown`), the node effects monitoring uses. Test: `BrowserMonitor.test.ts`.

## Progress
1. Fixed on next-release, uncommitted.
2. Chrome, a two-channel stream with the tone on the left, Mono 1+2 Mixed: direct and effects monitoring both L = R ×0.50 (direct was L ×1.00, R 0).
