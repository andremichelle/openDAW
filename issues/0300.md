## Summary
The reporter wants a group track in the timeline that holds other tracks. Goals: collapse the timeline, apply effects to several tracks at once by dragging them onto the group, and see routing directly in the timeline instead of hidden aux routing. André noted the track layout update already allows compacting, and that openDAW does not separate fx buses from output buses. The reporter accepted the compacting and stressed shared effects and visible routing. A later user (polinom) explained the folder idea with screenshots (viewed): Ableton group track folded and unfolded with children indented under "1 Group", and Reaper folders in three states (unfolded, slightly folded with a thin summary lane, almost hidden).
