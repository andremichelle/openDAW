# openDAW 1.1

## New Features

### Random Modulator: Bias and Focus
Bias sets the centre the random values gather around. Focus pulls the values towards that centre, from untouched at zero to the bias itself at full focus. Both are automatable and available in the scripting API.

_Source: openDAW_

### Mixer Device Rows
Every channel strip lists its MIDI effects above the input and its audio effects below it. Toggle a device, click its name to jump to it in the device panel, drag devices to reorder them or move them to other strips, and add new ones with the add button. Both sections can be collapsed to a device count.

_Source: openDAW_

### Compact Mixer Volume
When the mixer gets too short for the volume fader, each channel strip switches to a volume knob below the panning knob with a small stereo peak meter beside it. Faders and peak meters thin out their scale lines and dB labels as they get shorter.

_Source: openDAW_

### Playfield Pads Play Any Instrument
Every Playfield pad holds its own MIDI effects, its own instrument and its own audio effects, with gain, pan, mute, solo and exclude. Sample pads play with Nano, so every Nano control is available on each pad. An empty pad offers a sample button and an instrument button, which opens the device browser with presets, WebCLAPs and soundfonts. A pad only hears its own note. A Playfield, a Composite or Tape cannot go on a pad. Scripts put an instrument on a pad with addPad and read those pads back. Old projects and presets open with every slot turned into a pad with the same sample, envelope, pitch, mix, effects and automation.

_Source: [#288](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/issues/288) by kaiartikkas, [#411](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/issues/411)_

### Playfield Pads on the Timeline
A pad dragged onto the timeline, or moved with Move to Own Track, becomes its own track with its instrument, effects, mix, automation and only the notes that trigger it. Copy, Cut and Paste on a pad work like an audio unit: a copied pad pastes onto another pad, onto the timeline as a track or into a Composite as a layer, and any track pasted onto a pad brings its notes along on the pad's key. A track dragged by its instrument onto a pad becomes that pad the same way, with Alt it stays and the pad gets a copy.

_Source: [#409](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/issues/409) by kaiartikkas_

### Cubed Pattern Menu
Right-click anywhere on Cubed for Octave Up and Down, Double, Reverse and Invert around a typed note. A pattern exports to a note region or a note clip exactly as the sequencer plays it, and a selected region or clip imports back into the pattern.

_Source: [#352](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/issues/352) by BeatMaxPrediction_

### Chord Keys
A new MIDI effect that plays the chords of a key from single keys. Each white key plays the chord on its step of the key, and held black keys change it: C# adds the 7th, D# the 9th, F# and G# play sus2 and sus4, A# the next inversion. Pressed while a chord sounds, a black key adds or removes only its own note. It follows the project key, and Voice Leading plays each chord in the inversion closest to the previous one.

_Source: openDAW_

### Sampler
A new instrument that plays many samples by key and velocity. Each sample is a zone with its key range, velocity range, root key, tuning, gain, start, end and loop, which can run while the note is held or always, forwards or alternating. Zones live in groups with round robin, choke and release trigger. The panel has a filter, an amp and a mod envelope, two LFOs and a list of modulations from a source to a target. The sample zone window shows the map over a piano with tools to map by name, spread keys and velocities, crossfade and duplicate. SFZ and SF2 files import, the instrument exports as a soundfont, and scripts can build one through the scripting API.

_Source: [#538](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/issues/538)_

### Script Dialogs
A script can ask for values with openDAW.showDialog. It describes text, number, slider, checkbox and dropdown fields, the studio shows them in a dialog and hands the values back, or nothing when the dialog is cancelled.

_Source: openDAW_

### Surgeon
A new instrument that plays metallic FM themes for raw techno by itself while the transport runs. A pitch loop and four lanes grow from seeds and can mutate from pass to pass, and each lane can move any of the sixteen sound controls. Eight patterns switch on the bar, by a click or by automation. Off silences the loops, then Surgeon plays the notes of its track with the selected pattern's sound. The Roll buttons throw new pitches, lanes or sounds. Every pattern can play through a groove of its own, restart its loops every few steps and shift where they start. Every control is automatable and scripts reach all of it through the scripting API.

_Source: openDAW_

### openDrum
Ten new drum instruments that synthesize every hit without samples: Kick, Snare, Clap, Tom, Rim, Hat, Shaker, Cymbal, Bell and Perc. They play on a track or on a Playfield pad, where the pad's key sets the note. Each editor shows the hit its values make, its level and pitch over time and a dot for every hit that sounds, and pressing the display plays it. Mono cuts the ringing hit with each new note, Poly lets hits overlap, and Gate also cuts the hit when its note ends. Exclusive pads choke each other, so a closed hat cuts an open one. The Kick can add a rumbling tail that ducks under every hit, its length follows the song's tempo. Every value is automatable, and scripts create and edit all ten through the scripting API.

_Source: openDAW_

## Changes

### Samples Take Half the Space
A sample is stored as 16 bit when nothing is lost by it, which halves its size in the browser storage and in project bundles. The studio also keeps a sample in memory only while something shows or edits it. Samples stored earlier stay as they are until converted on the new Sample Optimisation page, reached from the storage page. It lists your own samples, tells which convert without loss, which by rounding and which stay 32 bit, and converts the selected ones.

_Source: openDAW_

### Audio Fade Handles
Hovering an audio clip shows larger fade handles in the clip colour. Drag a square to set the fade length, and the circle in the middle of the fade up or down to shape its curve. New fades start straight. Splitting a clip keeps the fade-in on the left piece and the fade-out on the right piece, with no fades at the cut. Holding Shift while dragging a handle moves it at a fifth of the speed and snaps near 25, 50 and 75 percent. Resting on a handle shows its value, which stays while dragging. The handles fade in and out when the pointer enters or leaves a clip.

_Source: pull requests [#547](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/pull/547), [#546](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/pull/546) and [#549](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/pull/549) by polinom_

### Clip Colours in the Context Menu
The context menu of clips and regions starts with the six clip colours as small squares. A click colours every selected clip in one undo step, and the current colour is outlined when all selected clips share it. Pressing on a clip, dragging onto a square and releasing works too. The Color submenu is gone.

_Source: pull request [#544](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/pull/544) by polinom_

### New Instruments Start Without a Region
A new instrument no longer creates a note region. An empty note lane shows the hint "Double-click to create a note-region" while Visible Help & Hints is on.

_Source: openDAW_

### Group Buses Fed by Sinks
In the mixer, a group bus without member tracks shows no collapse button and no group rail.

_Source: openDAW_

### Storage Assets by Kind
The Assets tab of the storage page lists samples, soundfonts and WebCLAPs as groups, each sorted by name. Files that cannot be read are listed below the summary with the project or asset they belong to.

_Source: openDAW_

## Fixes

### Soundfont Device Plays Zone Tuning
The Soundfont device ignored the coarse tune, fine tune and pitch correction of a soundfont, so instruments tuned that way played out of tune. It applies them now. Projects using such a soundfont sound different, now in tune.

_Source: [#550](https://github.com/andremichelle/openDAW/issues/550) by BeatMaxPrediction_

### Pasting an Audio Unit With Notes
Paste AudioUnit as Layer with Drop copied notes no longer fails silently when the pasted unit's regions hold notes.

_Source: openDAW_
