# openDrum Rim

Short, sharp hits from up to three ringing tones, cut by a short envelope and driven together, with a click and a
high-pass. It reaches rimshots, claves, woodblocks and side sticks.

---

## 0. Overview

_openDrum Rim_ plays one hit per note. The display shows the hit the current values make, with its level over time.

1. On a track, key 60 plays at Tune, every key above or below moves it by a semitone.
2. In a Playfield pad, every hit plays at Tune.
3. Every value can be automated.

Example uses:

1. A classic drum machine rimshot
2. Claves: one high tone, no drive
3. A hard, clipped rim for techno

---

## 1. Tones

1. **Tune**: the pitch of the first tone.
2. **Ratio**: the pitch of the second tone against the first.
3. **Balance**: the level of the second tone against the first.
4. **Ring**: how long the first tone rings.

---

## 2. High

1. **Level**: the level of a third, high tone.
2. **Ratio**: its pitch against the first tone.
3. **Ring**: how long the second tone rings, the third rings half as long.
4. **Swell**: over how many of their own cycles the tones swell in, so they ring rather than click.

---

## 3. Shape

1. **Decay**: how long the hit lasts.
2. **Punch**: the shape of the decay. Negative drops fast and lingers, positive holds and ends sooner.
3. **Drive**: drives the tones together, up to 36 dB.
4. **Hard**: turns the drive from soft saturation into a hard clip.

---

## 4. Strike

1. **Click**: the level of the click at the start, at the pitch of the third tone.
2. **Cut**: a high-pass that thins the hit.
3. **Restart**: On: every hit starts the tones at the same point and sounds the same. Off: every hit starts them
   somewhere else, which you hear once they are driven.

---

## 5. Play

1. **Volume**: the level of the device.
2. **Velocity**: how much the note's velocity changes the level.
3. **Mode**:
    1. **Mono**: a new note cuts the ringing one.
    2. **Poly**: hits ring on and overlap.
    3. **Gate**: as Mono, and the hit is cut short when its note ends.
