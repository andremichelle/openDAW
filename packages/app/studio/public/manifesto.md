# The openDAW Manifesto

Oct 9, 2026 · @André Michelle

openDAW is free, open source, and independent. This manifesto fixes those three facts for good. It binds every owner of openDAW, present and future, and no change of ownership can alter it.

## Why this exists

I started openDAW to democratize music production and to bring the process of making music back to the surface. High quality creation tools should be within reach of everyone, with a strong focus on education and data privacy.

## 1. Free, for everyone

- openDAW is free to use for every person, school, and institution, with no limit on time or features.
- No part of openDAW is ever sold, rented, or locked behind a subscription or an account.
- openDAW carries no ads.

## 2. Open source under AGPL v3

- The complete source code of openDAW, including the SDK, is published in a public repository under AGPL v3 (or later).
- Every future version is published under the same licence. The public repository is where openDAW is developed, not a delayed or reduced copy.

## 3. Respect for the people who use it

openDAW stands for radical simplicity and respect.

- No sign up
- No tracking
- No cookie banners
- No user profiling
- No terms and conditions
- No ads
- No paywalls
- No data mining

Projects, samples, and recordings belong to the people who made them. openDAW never sells them, to AI companies or to anyone else.

## 4. An advanced DAW with education at its heart

- openDAW remains a complete and sophisticated digital audio workstation. It is accessible because it is free and open, not because it is simplified.
- openDAW is never reduced to a demo, a trial, or an entry point for another product.
- openDAW keeps a strong focus on education and stays open to everyone who wants to make music.

## 5. Independent direction

- André Michelle, founder of openDAW, decides its features, roadmap, design, and alignment.
- The owner of openDAW may propose and fund work. It may not overrule these decisions or force features in or out.
- Should I step back, I name the person or body that takes over this role, with the same rights.

## 6. Permanent

- This manifesto is binding on whoever owns openDAW.
- It applies to every owner, successor, acquirer, or assignee of openDAW, its code, its name, or its domains.
- openDAW may only be transferred to a new owner who has first accepted this manifesto in writing.
- No owner can change, suspend, or revoke it.
- If an owner breaks this manifesto and does not repair the breach, the openDAW name, its domains, and all rights in its code return to the founder or to the steward he named.

André Michelle, founder of openDAW
